Option 1: eBird Trip Report (New & Encouraged for Route Leaders)

If your party uses eBird in the field, you no longer need to transcribe your numbers into Excel afterward. You can bundle all of your count-day checklists into a single Trip Report and email me the link.
How to submit via eBird:
1. Share checklists within your party: Have one person keep the checklist for each stop or section and share it with the rest of your group so sightings are not duplicated.
2. Create a Trip Report: Go to ebird.org/trips, click Create Trip Report, select [Count Date], and include all checklists from your assigned route.
3. Set Visibility: Set the report visibility to "Public" or "Anyone with the link" so I can view it.
4. Add Effort & Notes to the "Narrative" box: Please paste and fill out the following details in the Trip Report Narrative so I can log your official effort:
	• Route Number / Area Name:
	• Participant Names (all members):
	• Party Hours & Miles (Walking):
	• Party Hours & Miles (Driving):
	• Owling Hours & Miles (if applicable):
	• Feeder Watching Hours (if applicable):
	• Duplicate Birds: Please do not duplicate birds on multiple lists. However, if you have to, please note any duplications so I can adjust the final tally.
5. Email me the link to your Trip Report.
(For more tips on using eBird during the count, check out eBird's "eBirding on Christmas Bird Counts" guide. https://ebird.org/news/ebirding-on-christmas-bird-counts)

Option 2: Excel Tally Sheet (Attached)

If you prefer to stick with our traditional workflow, simply fill out the attached Excel spreadsheet as you have in previous years and email the completed file back to me.

Quick Reminders for Count Day

• Document unusual sightings: If you spot a rare or write-in species, please attach a photo to your eBird checklist and include detailed field notes in the report and the attached form.

Thank you again for your time and dedication to our local birds!
